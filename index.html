About

Meet Janine

She/Her/Hers

For nearly 20 years, I have provided treatment across private practice, community mental health centers, hospitals, schools, yoga studios, and in-home settings.

I combine counseling, yoga, and movement-based support to help clients build calmer patterns, stronger relationships, and more workable daily routines.

My practice is grounded in relational care, practical tools, and a steady therapeutic presence that adapts to each client or family.
