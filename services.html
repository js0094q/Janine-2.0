Mindful Movement

Yoga, Movement, & Co-Regulation

Personalized sessions for children, adolescents, adults, families, and groups.

Mindful movement provides an opportunity to explore mindfulness and movement strategies designed to increase calm, maintain focus, and energize the brain and body. Each session is designed to meet the unique needs of the client. Incorporating yoga, movement, mindfulness, and the principles of educational kinesiology yields powerful sessions that expand participants' ability to move through life's challenging moments.

What it can include

  • Family co-regulation strategies through movement-based activities and games
  • Specialized sessions for youth athletes targeting strength, mobility, and flexibility
  • Yoga-based mindfulness adapted for schools and corporate groups
  • Individual or small-group work that complements talk therapy
