HSHH Program

"Happy Spouse, Happy House"

Relationship skills, attachment awareness, and intentional communication for couples who want a steadier foundation.

The program is designed to help partners build connection, communication, and intention instead of leaving those pieces to chance.

Request more info See services

We often focus on curating the perfect wedding day, invitation, living space, or social media post with and about our partner. Yet, rarely do we consider the skills needed to create a life together that is grounded in connection, communication, and intention.

My goal is to provide a space for you to collaboratively build the foundation for a lasting partnership. Begin your journey with relational alignment so you can flourish individually and together.

Who can benefit?

Any partnership desiring to enhance connection, whether you are looking for a non-denominational premarital program or want to expand an existing relationship.

The Skills

The first 6 sessions focus on skill building - expanding introspection, understanding attachment, engaging in healthy communication, and enhancing connection.

The Lens

Sessions 7-12 focus on understanding the lens through which each partner sees the world, and what has shaped each partner's view of relationships.

The Result

Fewer misperceptions, clearer communication, more rewarding conversations, and the ability to navigate inevitable conflicts with more steadiness.
