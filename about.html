About

Meet Janine

Counseling, yoga, and movement-based support shaped by nearly two decades of work with individuals, couples, families, and groups.

The practice is built around steady presence, practical tools, and a collaborative approach that helps clients move forward at a workable pace.

Call (201) 282-6720 See services

For nearly 20 years, I have provided treatment across private practice, community mental health centers, hospitals, schools, yoga studios, and in-home settings.

I obtained my B.A. in Psychology from Rutgers University, followed by graduate degrees in both Psychological Studies (M.A.) and Marriage and Family Therapy (Ed.S.) from Seton Hall University. I am a Licensed Professional Counselor in the State of New Jersey. I am also a certified adult and youth yoga instructor. That combination of clinical training, movement, and stress understanding supports the work I do with clients.

As a private practice owner, I provide individual, couple, and family treatment, along with yoga-based wellness programming and private mindfulness coaching. My therapeutic services are customized to the specific needs of each client or family.
